Under the hood

Simple for the user. Intelligent underneath.

Instead of hard-coded recommendation buttons, Shopie Guide combines conversational AI with a structured product knowledge source.

01

Understand

The assistant interprets natural-language requests such as budget, category, brand, use case and specific requirements.

02

Retrieve

Shopie Guide searches the connected ProductCatalog to find products that can be verified from the project's structured data.

03

Recommend

The assistant ranks relevant options and explains why they match the user's requirements, including price, rating and important specifications.

Responsible recommendations

Useful AI with clear boundaries.

Shopie Guide is designed to stay grounded in its available catalog and clearly communicate the limits of demonstration marketplace data.

Catalog-grounded answers

Product recommendations are based on the connected ProductCatalog rather than invented products or unsupported product claims.

✓ Product name and brand
✓ Price and rating
✓ Relevant specifications
✓ Requirement-aware recommendations

Demo data, not live marketplace data

The catalog contains indicative demonstration data. Prices, ratings, reviews and availability should not be treated as live Amazon, Flipkart or other marketplace information.

✓ Transparent data limitations
✓ No invented purchasing links
✓ No false claims of live inventory
